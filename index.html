About

Product Designer with 4+ years in UX/UI, the last 2+ in enterprise B2B SaaS and developer tools at SmartBear. I design complex, data-heavy workflows end to end, and the design systems that let teams ship them consistently.

I led the UI reskin of ReadyAPI within the SmartBear Design System, work our CEO and CTPO recognised as high-impact. Today I co-lead the refactor of our design system so AI agents can read and apply it, using Figma MCP, Claude Code and GitHub. I prototype in HTML with Claude Code to validate ideas with stakeholders and users before anything gets built.

I work closely with UX researchers, run design meetings and make decisions directly with PMs and engineers. I mentored a junior designer and lead a knowledge-sharing initiative across Design, Research and Technical Writing. Promoted from Designer I to Designer II in 2025 and awarded the Curious Award 2025.

Earlier: web and e-commerce design, style guides and UX audits at Salestube powered by hmmh. BA in Graphic Design, SWPS University, Wrocław. Polish (native), English (C1).
